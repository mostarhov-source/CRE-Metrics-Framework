# 📘 Aggregated Business Outcomes (ABOs)

This file defines Aggregated Business Outcomes (ABOs) for Industrial & Logistics Commercial Real Estate (I&L CRE).  
Each ABO represents a high-level value outcome that CRE organisations aim to achieve, supported by Business Outcome–Driven Metrics (BODMs) and Technology-Outcome Driven Metrics (TODMs).  

---

## 💰 1. Optimised Portfolio Returns

**Definition:**  
Improved financial performance and return on investment across the CRE portfolio, from acquisition through disposition.  

**Why It Matters:**  
- Core objective of asset management and investment strategy.  
- Directly linked to investor confidence and ability to raise capital.  
- Guides buy/hold/sell decisions at the portfolio level.  

**Example Indicators:**  
- IRR, NPV, Equity Multiple, NOI growth.  
- Portfolio yield vs benchmark index.  
- Asset-level return contribution to overall fund.  

**Example Evidence / Benchmarks:**  
- Core CRE portfolios target 8–12% IRR.  
- Opportunistic / value-add portfolios often target >15%.  

---

## ⚙️ 2. Enhanced Operational Efficiency & Sustainability

**Definition:**  
Improved efficiency of day-to-day operations while reducing environmental footprint through energy, water, and waste optimisation.  

**Why It Matters:**  
- Lowers operating costs and improves net income.  
- Meets investor and regulatory ESG requirements.  
- Supports tenant retention by providing efficient, sustainable buildings.  

**Example Indicators:**  
- Energy intensity (kWh/m²).  
- Peak demand reduction.  
- Carbon emissions per property.  
- Waste diversion rate.  

**Example Evidence / Benchmarks:**  
- Typical AI-driven energy optimisation: 10–20% energy savings.  
- ESG leaders target Net Zero operations by 2040–2050.  

---

## 🛡️ 3. Reduced Investment Risk & Improved Compliance

**Definition:**  
Minimisation of financial, operational, and compliance risks across the property lifecycle.  

**Why It Matters:**  
- Protects NOI stability and investor trust.  
- Ensures portfolio resilience against market volatility.  
- Avoids penalties and reputational damage from non-compliance.  

**Example Indicators:**  
- Loan-to-Value (LTV), Debt Yield.  
- Fault detection rate.  
- Lease data integrity %.  
- Compliance with local planning policy.  

**Example Evidence / Benchmarks:**  
- Core CRE lending typically capped at 60% LTV.  
- Fault detection systems aim for ≥90% detection rate.  

---

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 4. Increased Tenant Satisfaction & Retention

**Definition:**  
Higher tenant loyalty, reduced churn, and improved tenant experience across the portfolio.  

**Why It Matters:**  
- Tenant renewals stabilise cash flow.  
- Strong satisfaction drives reputation and leasing velocity.  
- Directly impacts occupancy and rent growth.  

**Example Indicators:**  
- Tenant Retention Rate.  
- Tenant Payment Reliability Index (TPRI).  
- Market Rent vs. Contract Rent.  
- Occupancy cost % of tenant revenue.  

**Example Evidence / Benchmarks:**  
- CRE portfolios typically target ≥80% tenant retention.  
- TPRI healthy range: ≥85 index value.  

---

## 🧠 5. Streamlined Decision-Making & Workflow

**Definition:**  
Faster, more accurate decision-making through digitisation, automation, and analytics.  

**Why It Matters:**  
- Reduces reliance on manual reporting and spreadsheets.  
- Accelerates responsiveness to market changes.  
- Enhances collaboration across investment, leasing, and operations teams.  

**Example Indicators:**  
- Report generation time reduction %.  
- Financial dashboard automation %.  
- Data integration rate %.  
- Data model alignment accuracy %.  

**Example Evidence / Benchmarks:**  
- Leaders in CRE aim for ≥90% of reporting fully automated.  
- Report generation time reduced by ≥50% in digital-first firms.
