# 📑 Technology-Outcome Driven Metrics (TODMs)

This file defines Technology-Outcome Driven Metrics (TODMs) for Industrial & Logistics Commercial Real Estate (I&L CRE).  
Each TODM represents a measurable technical capability outcome that enables business value creation.  

---

## ⚡ 1. Energy Savings %
**Category:** Building Systems / ESG Tech  
**Definition:** The percentage reduction in energy consumption achieved through deployment of AI-powered building management systems, IoT sensors, or HVAC optimisation compared to baseline.  
**Why It Matters:** Demonstrates cost savings, supports ESG compliance, and validates ROI from PropTech investments.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** (Baseline Consumption – Current Consumption) ÷ Baseline Consumption × 100  
**Data Sources:** BMS telemetry, IoT meters, energy bills, tariff data.  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** 10–20% typical; up to 25% in AI pilots (e.g., BrainBox AI).  

---

## 📈 2. Data Capture Automation Rate %
**Category:** Data Management / Automation  
**Definition:** Percentage of operational data points (e.g., invoices, meter readings, goods received notes) captured automatically via sensors, APIs, RPA, or computer vision.  
**Why It Matters:** Improves data quality, timeliness, and supports AI analytics.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** Automated Data Captures ÷ Total Data Captures × 100  
**Data Sources:** API logs, IoT sensors, RPA logs, CV systems.  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** Baseline <30%; Target ≥80%; Leaders >95%.  

---

## 🖥️ 3. Uptime / Availability of Automated Systems
**Category:** IT Infrastructure / Operations  
**Definition:** The percentage of time that AI/automation systems (e.g., BMS optimisation, IoT monitoring, RPA bots) are fully operational.  
**Why It Matters:** High uptime ensures reliability of automation investments and prevents downtime costs.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** (System Uptime Hours ÷ Total Hours) × 100  
**Data Sources:** System logs, monitoring dashboards, SLA reports.  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** ≥99.5% uptime for critical systems.  

---

## 🏗️ 4. AI-Generated Development Options per Assessment
**Category:** Development Tech / Automation  
**Definition:** Number of compliant design or site development options generated by AI tools (e.g., Blocktype, SiteSolve) per site appraisal.  
**Why It Matters:** Accelerates feasibility studies, reduces reliance on manual planners.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** Total AI-Generated Options ÷ Number of Assessments  
**Data Sources:** AI planning tools, system logs.  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** ≥5 compliant options per appraisal.  

---

## 📊 5. Accuracy of AI-Generated Cost Estimates
**Category:** Development Tech / AI  
**Definition:** Degree of alignment between AI-generated cost estimates and actual project costs.  
**Why It Matters:** Validates reliability of AI planning tools for financial forecasting.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** 1 – (|AI Estimate – Actual Cost| ÷ Actual Cost)  
**Data Sources:** AI estimate logs, actual project costs (ERP).  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** ≥90% accuracy.  

---

## 🧾 6. Accuracy of Financial Models
**Category:** Finance Tech / Modelling  
**Definition:** Accuracy of automated financial models compared against actual performance (e.g., NOI, IRR).  
**Why It Matters:** Ensures automated valuations are trustworthy.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** 1 – (|Model Output – Actual Performance| ÷ Actual Performance)  
**Data Sources:** ERP, BI dashboards, market comps.  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** ≥95% accuracy.  

---

## 🔄 7. Frequency of Automated Sell/Hold Recommendations
**Category:** Investment Analytics / Automation  
**Definition:** Number of sell/hold recommendations produced by AI or automated financial systems per property per year.  
**Why It Matters:** Improves decision cadence and investor responsiveness.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** Total Automated Recommendations ÷ Total Properties ÷ Time Period  
**Data Sources:** Investment platforms, portfolio analytics.  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** ≥1 per quarter per property.  

---

## 🏭 8. Discrepancy Detection Rate (Plans vs Site)
**Category:** Construction / Computer Vision  
**Definition:** Percentage of discrepancies between architects’ plans and as-built conditions automatically flagged by AI/computer vision.  
**Why It Matters:** Reduces costly rework and ensures compliance.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** Discrepancies Detected ÷ Total Discrepancies × 100  
**Data Sources:** CV scans, BIM models, inspection reports.  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** ≥90%.  

---

## 📡 9. Lease Data Integrity %
**Category:** Lease Management / Data Quality  
**Definition:** % of lease records validated against authoritative sources (ERP, legal documents).  
**Why It Matters:** Ensures accuracy of rent rolls and financial reporting.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** Validated Lease Records ÷ Total Lease Records × 100  
**Data Sources:** Lease DB, ERP, scanned lease docs.  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** ≥95%.  

---

## 📅 10. Rent Review Automation Rate %
**Category:** Lease Management / Workflow Automation  
**Definition:** % of rent review processes (calculations, reminders, approvals) executed automatically.  
**Why It Matters:** Reduces errors, speeds up cycles.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** Automated Rent Reviews ÷ Total Rent Reviews × 100  
**Data Sources:** Workflow logs, document automation.  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** ≥80%.  

---

## ⏱️ 11. Lease Event Processing Time Reduction %
**Category:** Lease Management / Efficiency  
**Definition:** % reduction in average time to process lease events (renewals, expiries, notices).  
**Why It Matters:** Prevents missed deadlines, improves tenant experience.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** (Baseline Time – Current Time) ÷ Baseline × 100  
**Data Sources:** Case management logs, workflow data.  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** ≥30% faster.  

---

## 🏢 12. Vacancy Data Accuracy %
**Category:** Portfolio Data / Quality  
**Definition:** % of reported vacancies matching actual occupancy status.  
**Why It Matters:** Accurate vacancy data supports valuations and rent forecasting.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** Correct Vacancy Records ÷ Total Records × 100  
**Data Sources:** Lease DB, CRM, property mgmt system.  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** ≥98%.  

---

## 👥 13. Tenant Record Duplication Rate %
**Category:** CRM / Data Quality  
**Definition:** % of duplicate tenant/customer entries across systems.  
**Why It Matters:** Avoids confusion, supports accurate reporting.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** Duplicate Records ÷ Total Records × 100  
**Data Sources:** CRM, lease DB.  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** ≤1%.  

---

## 📊 14. Financial Dashboard Automation %
**Category:** Reporting / BI Automation  
**Definition:** % of dashboards automatically generated from live system data.  
**Why It Matters:** Reduces lag, ensures decisions are data-driven.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** Automated Dashboards ÷ Total Dashboards × 100  
**Data Sources:** BI platform logs, ERP integrations.  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** 100% for core reports.  

---

## 📉 15. Report Generation Time Reduction %
**Category:** Reporting / Efficiency  
**Definition:** % reduction in time required to generate standard reports.  
**Why It Matters:** Saves staff time, accelerates decision-making.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** (Baseline Time – Current Time) ÷ Baseline × 100  
**Data Sources:** User logs, reporting platforms.  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** ≥50%.  

---

## 🗂️ 16. Asset Hierarchy Data Coverage %
**Category:** Portfolio Data / Quality  
**Definition:** % of assets with a fully digitised hierarchy (portfolio → property → unit → lease → tenant).  
**Why It Matters:** Ensures no gaps in portfolio visibility.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** Fully Digitised Assets ÷ Total Assets × 100  
**Data Sources:** ERP, property mgmt DB.  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** ≥95%.  

---

## 🔗 17. Property Data Integration Rate %
**Category:** Data Management / Integration  
**Definition:** % of property-level data sources integrated into a central BI/ERP environment.  
**Why It Matters:** Reduces silos, improves reporting consistency.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** Integrated Systems ÷ Total Systems × 100  
**Data Sources:** Integration logs, data warehouse.  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** ≥90%.  

---

## 🌱 18. Green Lease Compliance Automation %
**Category:** ESG / Lease Management  
**Definition:** % of green lease clauses tracked automatically (e.g., energy use, recycling obligations).  
**Why It Matters:** Demonstrates ESG compliance to investors and regulators.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** Automated Green Clauses ÷ Total Green Clauses × 100  
**Data Sources:** Lease DB, ESG reporting platform.  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** ≥80%.  

---

## 📐 19. Data Model Alignment Accuracy %
**Category:** Data Governance / Quality  
**Definition:** % of eForms, reports, and dashboards aligned with the master CRE data model.  
**Why It Matters:** Prevents data inconsistencies between platforms.  
**Formula / Measurement Approach:** Aligned Forms ÷ Total Forms × 100  
**Data Sources:** BI configs, data governance logs.  
**Example Targets / Benchmarks:** ≥95%.

### Cybersecurity Incident Rate  
**Category:** IT Infrastructure Operations  
**Definition:** The number of detected cybersecurity breaches or fraud incidents per year within CRE IT systems.  
**Why It Matters:** Minimizes disruptions, data loss, and reputational damage impacting operations and investor confidence.  
**Formula:** Number of Incidents Reported / Total Systems Monitored × 100  
**Data Sources:** IT security logs, incident reports, audit trails.  
**Example Targets:** <1 incident per year per 100 monitored systems; improved vulnerability patching.

---

### IoT Sensor Health & Data Quality  
**Category:** Building Systems ESG Tech  
**Definition:** Percentage of operational IoT sensors reporting valid, high-quality data continuously as expected.  
**Why It Matters:** Ensures reliability of data streams for analytics, automation, and decision-making.  
**Formula:** Valid Sensor Reports / Total Installed Sensors × 100  
**Data Sources:** Sensor telemetry logs, monitoring dashboards.  
**Example Targets:** 98% sensor uptime and data validity.

---

### Digital Twin Fidelity  
**Category:** Data Management Integration  
**Definition:** Degree to which digital twin models reflect real-time, accurate asset and portfolio status.  
**Why It Matters:** Supports precise simulation, predictive analytics, and asset management decisions.  
**Formula:** Number of Accurate Twin Updates / Total Updates Scheduled × 100  
**Data Sources:** BIM updates, IoT data feeds, systems integration logs.  
**Example Targets:** >95% update accuracy and timeliness.

---

### Technology Adoption Rate  
**Category:** Organizational Capability  
**Definition:** Percentage of staff and portfolio assets actively using digital and automated platforms.  
**Why It Matters:** Measures digital transformation maturity and readiness for new technology-driven opportunities.  
**Formula:** Users or Assets Using Tech / Total Users or Assets × 100  
**Data Sources:** User access logs, platform usage reports.  
**Example Targets:** 80%+ adoption within 12 months of rollout.

---

### PropTech Investment ROI  
**Category:** Finance Tech Modelling  
**Definition:** Financial return attributed directly to PropTech investments, relative to investment cost.  
**Why It Matters:** Validates technology spend effectiveness and supports continuous innovation funding.  
**Formula:** (Revenue or Cost Savings from PropTech - Investment Cost) / Investment Cost × 100  
**Data Sources:** Financial reports, PropTech project budgets, cost savings analyses.  
**Example Targets:** 20% ROI within first 2 years post deployment.

---

### Tenant Experience Tech Utilization  
**Category:** Tenant Outcomes  
**Definition:** Percentage of tenants actively engaging with technology platforms e.g., apps or portals for services and feedback.  
**Why It Matters:** Enhances tenant satisfaction, retention, and operational responsiveness.  
**Formula:** Active Tenant Platform Users / Total Tenants × 100  
**Data Sources:** App usage logs, tenant surveys, CRM data.  
**Example Targets:** 70% active usage rate within first 6 months.
