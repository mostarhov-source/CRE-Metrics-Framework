# Value Chain: Productivity & Workflow Efficiency

## Primary Value Lever (PVL)

**Productivity & Workflow Efficiency** — Improvements in the speed, accuracy, and effectiveness of business processes, supported by digitisation and automation.

## Aggregated Business Outcomes (ABO)

- **Streamlined Decision-Making & Workflow** — Faster, more accurate decision-making through digitisation, automation, and analytics.
