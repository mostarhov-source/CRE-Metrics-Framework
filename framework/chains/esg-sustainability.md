# Value Chain: ESG & Sustainability

## Primary Value Lever (PVL)

**ESG & Sustainability** — Enhancing environmental performance, social responsibility, and governance transparency in line with regulatory and investor expectations.

## Aggregated Business Outcomes (ABO)

- **Enhanced Operational Efficiency & Sustainability** — Lower operating costs, ESG compliance, tenant retention through efficient buildings.

## Business Outcome-Driven Metrics (BODM)

| Metric | timing | cadence | Target | Formula |
|--------|--------|---------|--------|---------|
| kWh/m² Reduction | lead | monthly | ≥10% | (Baseline – Current) ÷ Baseline × 100 |
| Peak Demand Reduction | lead | monthly | ≥5–10% | (Baseline Peak – Current) ÷ Baseline × 100 |
| Carbon Emissions Reduction | lag | annual | Net Zero by 2040–2050 | (Baseline – Current) ÷ Baseline × 100 |
| Waste Reduction % | lag | quarterly | ≥25% over 5 years | (Baseline Waste – Current) ÷ Baseline × 100 |
| Green Lease Compliance Automation % | lead | monthly | ≥80% | Automated Clauses ÷ Total Clauses × 100 |
| Climate Risk Exposure | lag | annual | <10% portfolio in high-risk | Weighted risk scores by asset value |
| Embodied Carbon Intensity | lag | per-project | 20% reduction over 5 years | Total Embodied Carbon ÷ Gross Floor Area |
| Construction Waste Reduction % | lag | per-project | ≥15% | (Baseline Waste – Current) ÷ Baseline × 100 |

## Technology-Outcome Driven Metrics (TODM)

| Metric | timing | cadence | Target | Enables |
|--------|--------|---------|--------|---------|
| Energy Savings % | lead | monthly | 10–20% | kWh/m² Reduction, Peak Demand Reduction |
| Green Lease Compliance Automation % | lead | monthly | ≥80% | Green Lease Compliance Automation % (BODM) |
| Digital Twin Fidelity | lead | quarterly | >95% | Carbon Emissions Reduction |

> **Note:** Waste Reduction %, Embodied Carbon Intensity, and Construction Waste Reduction % are tracked through waste management records and life-cycle assessment databases rather than AI/PropTech tools. These have no TODM enablers in this chain.

## Example Value Chain

```
ESG & Sustainability (PVL)
  ↓
Enhanced Operational Efficiency & Sustainability (ABO)
  ↓
Carbon Emissions reduced 18% (BODM)
  ↓
Energy Savings % = 18% via AI BMS (TODM)
  ↓
Net Zero pathway confirmed, EPC B achieved
```
