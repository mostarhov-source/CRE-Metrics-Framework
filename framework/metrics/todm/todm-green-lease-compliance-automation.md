# Green Lease Compliance Automation %

**Category:** ESG / Lease Management
**Definition:** % of green lease clauses tracked automatically via lease management and ESG platforms.

**Why It Matters:** Demonstrates ESG compliance to investors and regulators without manual monitoring.

**Formula:** Automated Green Clauses ÷ Total Green Clauses × 100

**Data Sources:** Lease DB, ESG reporting platform.

**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥80%.
