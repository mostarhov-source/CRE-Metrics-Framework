# Discrepancy Detection Rate (Plans vs Site)

**Category:** Construction / Computer Vision
**Definition:** Percentage of discrepancies between architects' plans and as-built conditions automatically flagged by AI or computer vision.

**Why It Matters:** Reduces costly rework and ensures construction compliance with approved designs.

**Formula:** Discrepancies Detected ÷ Total Discrepancies × 100

**Data Sources:** Computer vision scans, BIM models, inspection reports.

**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥90%.

**Enables:** Compliance with Architects' Plans %
