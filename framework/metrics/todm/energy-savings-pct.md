# Energy Savings %

**Category:** Building Systems / ESG Tech
**Definition:** The percentage reduction in energy consumption achieved through AI-powered building management systems, IoT sensors, or HVAC optimisation compared to baseline.

**Why It Matters:** Demonstrates cost savings, supports ESG compliance, and validates ROI from PropTech investments.

**Formula:** (Baseline Consumption – Current Consumption) ÷ Baseline Consumption × 100

**Data Sources:** BMS telemetry, IoT meters, energy bills, tariff data.

**Example Target / Benchmarks:** 10–20% typical; up to 25% in AI pilots (e.g., BrainBox AI).

**Enables:** kWh/m² Reduction, Peak Demand Reduction
