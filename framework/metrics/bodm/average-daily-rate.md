# Average Daily Rate (ADR)

**Category:** Tenant Outcomes
**Definition:** Average rental revenue per day per unit, primarily used for flex-industrial assets.

**Why It Matters:** Benchmark for short-term and flex asset pricing strategy.

**Formula:** Total Rental Revenue ÷ Total Unit-Days Sold

**Data Sources:** Booking/reservation system, ERP
