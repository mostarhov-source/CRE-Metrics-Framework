# Construction Waste Reduction %

**Category:** ESG
**Definition:** % reduction in waste generated during construction and refurbishment.

**Why It Matters:** Aligns construction activities with ESG targets and reduces disposal costs.

**Formula:** (Baseline Waste – Current) ÷ Baseline × 100

**Data Sources:** Waste management records

**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥15%
