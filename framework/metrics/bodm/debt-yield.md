# Debt Yield

**Category:** Financial Performance
**Definition:** NOI divided by loan amount, showing lender's risk exposure.

**Why It Matters:** Indicates ability to service debt independent of cap rates.

**Formula:** NOI ÷ Loan Amount

**Data Sources:** NOI reports, ERP loan data.

**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥10% typical minimum.
