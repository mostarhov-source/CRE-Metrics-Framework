# kWh/m² Reduction

**Category:** ESG
**Definition:** Change in average energy consumption per square metre.

**Why It Matters:** Tracks efficiency improvements and ESG compliance across the portfolio.

**Formula:** (Baseline kWh/m² – Current kWh/m²) ÷ Baseline × 100

**Data Sources:** BMS, Energy meters, Utility bills

**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥10% reduction
