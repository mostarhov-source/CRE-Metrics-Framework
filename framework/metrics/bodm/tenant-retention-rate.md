# Tenant Retention Rate

**Category:** Tenant Outcomes
**Definition:** % of tenants renewing leases versus total expiring leases.

**Why It Matters:** Critical to stabilising NOI — tenant churn directly impacts income continuity.

**Formula:** Renewed Leases ÷ Expiring Leases × 100

**Data Sources:** Lease DB, CRM

**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥80%
