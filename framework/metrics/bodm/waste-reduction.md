# Waste Reduction %

**Category:** ESG
**Definition:** % reduction in waste generated from operations.

**Why It Matters:** Improves ESG scores and cuts waste disposal costs.

**Formula:** (Baseline Waste – Current) ÷ Baseline × 100

**Data Sources:** Waste transfer notes, Facility logs

**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥25% over 5 years
