# Tenant Utilization Rate

**Category:** Tenant Outcomes
**Definition:** Proportion of leased space actively used by tenants.

**Why It Matters:** Assesses space efficiency and tenant satisfaction, guiding portfolio optimisation.

**Formula:** Actual Occupied Space ÷ Leased Space × 100

**Data Sources:** Occupancy sensors, Access control, Tenant reports

**Example Target / Benchmarks:** >75% in flexible/office spaces
