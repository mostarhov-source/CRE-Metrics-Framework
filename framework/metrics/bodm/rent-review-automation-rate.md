# Rent Review Automation Rate %

**Category:** Operational Efficiency
**Definition:** % of rent review processes (calculations, reminders, approvals) automated.

**Why It Matters:** Reduces errors and accelerates review cycles.

**Formula:** Automated Reviews ÷ Total Reviews × 100

**Data Sources:** Workflow automation logs.

**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥80%.
