# Climate Risk Exposure

**Category:** ESG
**Definition:** Quantitative assessment of portfolio exposure to physical climate risks such as flooding, heatwaves, and storms.

**Why It Matters:** Enables targeted risk mitigation and resilience planning to protect asset value and income stability.

**Formula:** Aggregated risk scores weighted by asset value or footprint

**Data Sources:** Climate models, GIS data, Insurance claims, Asset registries

**Example Target / Benchmarks:** Limit high-risk assets to <10% of portfolio value
