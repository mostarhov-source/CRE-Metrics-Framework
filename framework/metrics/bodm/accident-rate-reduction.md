# Accident Rate Reduction %

**Category:** Construction
**Definition:** % reduction in site accidents versus baseline.

**Why It Matters:** Improves worker safety, reduces liability, and ensures regulatory compliance.

**Formula:** (Baseline Accidents – Current) ÷ Baseline × 100

**Data Sources:** Safety incident logs

**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥25%
