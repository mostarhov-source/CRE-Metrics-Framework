# Construction Rework Reduction %

**Category:** Construction
**Definition:** % reduction in construction rework versus baseline.

**Why It Matters:** Cuts costs and schedule delays caused by defective work.

**Formula:** (Baseline Rework – Current) ÷ Baseline × 100

**Data Sources:** QA logs, Site management systems

**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥20%
