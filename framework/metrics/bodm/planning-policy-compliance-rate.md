# Compliance Rate with Local Planning Policy

**Category:** Development
**Definition:** % of proposed development options compliant with local planning policy.

**Why It Matters:** Reduces planning risk and delays in the development pipeline.

**Formula:** Compliant Options ÷ Total Options × 100

**Data Sources:** Planning DB, Site assessments

**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥95%
