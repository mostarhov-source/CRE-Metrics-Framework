# Embodied Carbon Intensity

**Category:** ESG
**Definition:** Total embodied carbon emissions per square metre in construction or refurbishment projects.

**Why It Matters:** Extends sustainability focus beyond operations to lifecycle impacts, supporting net-zero goals.

**Formula:** Total Embodied Carbon (kg CO₂e) ÷ Gross Floor Area (m²)

**Data Sources:** Design data, Life-cycle assessment databases, Construction records

**Example Target / Benchmarks:** 20% reduction over 5 years
