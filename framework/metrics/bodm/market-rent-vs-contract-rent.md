# Market Rent vs Contract Rent

**Category:** Tenant Outcomes
**Definition:** Ratio of contract rent to market rent, expressed as a percentage.

**Why It Matters:** Identifies under- or over-rented units and informs rent review strategy.

**Formula:** Contract Rent ÷ Market Rent × 100

**Data Sources:** Lease DB, Market comparables

**Example Target / Benchmarks:** 90–110%
