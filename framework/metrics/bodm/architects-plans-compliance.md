# Compliance with Architects' Plans %

**Category:** Construction
**Definition:** % of construction works executed in line with approved architects' plans.

**Why It Matters:** Reduces risk of defects, disputes, and costly remediation.

**Formula:** Compliant Works ÷ Total Works × 100

**Data Sources:** Site inspections, Digital twins

**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥95%
