# Market Absorption Rate

**Category:** Market Performance
**Definition:** Percentage of available leasable or saleable space absorbed over a specified period.

**Why It Matters:** Indicates market demand and leasing success, critical for revenue growth planning.

**Formula:** Leased or Sold Area ÷ Total Available Area × 100

**Data Sources:** Lease management systems, Brokerage reports, CRM databases

**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥85% absorption within 12 months in core markets
