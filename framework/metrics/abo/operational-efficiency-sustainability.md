# Enhanced Operational Efficiency & Sustainability

**Category:** Aggregated Business Outcome
**Definition:** Improved efficiency of day-to-day operations while reducing environmental footprint through energy, water, and waste optimisation.

**Why It Matters:**
- Lowers operating costs and improves net income.
- Meets investor and regulatory ESG requirements.
- Supports tenant retention by providing efficient, sustainable buildings.

**Example Indicators:**
- Energy intensity (kWh/m²).
- Peak demand reduction.
- Carbon emissions per property.
- Waste diversion rate.

**Example Evidence / Benchmarks:**
- Typical AI-driven energy optimisation: 10–20% energy savings.
- ESG leaders target Net Zero operations by 2040–2050.
