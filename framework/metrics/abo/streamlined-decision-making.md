# Streamlined Decision-Making & Workflow

**Category:** Aggregated Business Outcome
**Definition:** Faster, more accurate decision-making through digitisation, automation, and analytics.

**Why It Matters:**
- Reduces reliance on manual reporting and spreadsheets.
- Accelerates responsiveness to market changes.
- Enhances collaboration across investment, leasing, and operations teams.

**Example Indicators:**
- Report generation time reduction %.
- Financial dashboard automation %.
- Data integration rate %.
- Data model alignment accuracy %.

**Example Evidence / Benchmarks:**
- Leaders in CRE aim for ≥90% of reporting fully automated.
- Report generation time reduced by ≥50% in digital-first firms.
