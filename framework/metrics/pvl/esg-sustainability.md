# ESG & Sustainability

**Category:** Primary Value Lever
**Definition:** Enhancing environmental performance, social responsibility, and governance transparency in line with regulatory and investor expectations.

**Why It Matters:**
- Increasingly critical for capital access and valuation premiums.
- Tenants demand sustainable, efficient spaces.
- Regulators impose stricter standards on emissions and reporting.

**Example Applications in CRE:**
- Reducing carbon emissions through smart building tech.
- Implementing green leases with automated compliance.
- Transparent ESG reporting to investors.

**Example Evidence / Benchmarks:**
- Net Zero targets by 2040–2050.
- Top CRE funds require ≥80% of assets with valid EPC B or better.
