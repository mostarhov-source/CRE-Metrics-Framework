# Cost Savings

**Category:** Primary Value Lever
**Definition:** Reduction in operating, capital, or administrative costs without compromising service quality.

**Why It Matters:**
- Improves NOI margins.
- Releases capital for reinvestment.
- Supports ESG objectives through efficiency.

**Example Applications in CRE:**
- AI-driven HVAC optimisation reducing energy bills.
- Group procurement for facilities services.
- Preventive maintenance reducing unplanned repair costs.

**Example Evidence / Benchmarks:**
- Energy efficiency programmes deliver 10–20% utility savings.
- Predictive maintenance reduces OPEX by 10–15%.
