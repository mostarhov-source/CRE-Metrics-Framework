# Risk Reduction

**Category:** Primary Value Lever
**Definition:** Mitigation of financial, operational, compliance, and reputational risks that threaten asset or portfolio performance.

**Why It Matters:**
- Ensures stable returns and investor confidence.
- Avoids costly regulatory fines or litigation.
- Enhances resilience against market volatility.

**Example Applications in CRE:**
- Monitoring tenant payment reliability.
- Maintaining healthy Loan-to-Value ratios.
- Ensuring building safety and regulatory compliance.

**Example Evidence / Benchmarks:**
- Core CRE lending limits typically ≤60% LTV.
- Payment default rates healthy if ≤2%.
