# 📘 Knowledge Base Index – Metrics Model (CRE I&L)

This index provides an overview of the **metrics model** that describes the value triangle for Industrial & Logistics Commercial Real Estate (I&L CRE).  
It connects **Primary Value Levers (PVL)** → **Aggregated Business Outcomes (ABO)** → **Business Outcome–Driven Metrics (BODM)** → **Technology-Outcome Driven Metrics (TODM)**.  

---

## 📂 Core Metrics Files

- **PVL_all.md – Primary Value Levers**  
  Defines the fundamental drivers of business value creation in CRE:  
  - Revenue Growth  
  - Cost Savings  
  - Risk Reduction  
  - ESG & Sustainability  
  - Productivity & Workflow Efficiency  

- **ABO_all.md – Aggregated Business Outcomes**  
  Captures the high-level strategic outcomes CRE firms aim to achieve:  
  - Optimised Portfolio Returns  
  - Enhanced Operational Efficiency & Sustainability  
  - Reduced Investment Risk & Improved Compliance  
  - Increased Tenant Satisfaction & Retention  
  - Streamlined Decision-Making & Workflow  

- **BODM_all.md – Business Outcome–Driven Metrics**  
  Contains the metrics that measure business outcomes (financial, operational, ESG, tenant, development). Examples:  
  - IRR, NOI, NPV  
  - Tenant Retention Rate  
  - TPRI (Tenant Payment Reliability Index)  
  - kWh/m² Reduction  

- **TODM_all.md – Technology-Outcome Driven Metrics**  
  Captures metrics that measure how PropTech and digital systems enable outcomes. Examples:  
  - Energy Savings %  
  - Lease Data Integrity %  
  - Rent Review Automation Rate %  
  - Data Capture Automation %
