# 📘 Supplementary Knowledge – CRE Metrics Framework

This file provides background context, frameworks, and methodology that support the knowledge base of metrics (ABO, BODM, TODM, PVL).  
It is not a catalogue of metrics, but a **reference guide** for understanding how the different knowledge files interconnect.  

---

## 🏗 Framework Overview

The knowledge base is aligned with the **Gartner Value Pyramid** for outcome-driven metrics in Commercial Real Estate (CRE).  

- **Primary Value Levers (PVLs):** Fundamental drivers of value creation (Revenue Growth, Cost Savings, Risk Reduction, ESG, Productivity).  
- **Aggregated Business Outcomes (ABOs):** High-level value outcomes for CRE portfolios (Optimised Returns, Efficiency, Compliance, Tenant Satisfaction, Decision-Making).  
- **Business Outcome–Driven Metrics (BODMs):** Metrics that measure progress toward ABOs (financial ratios, retention rates, ESG performance).  
- **Technology-Outcome Driven Metrics (TODMs):** Technical measures of how digital systems and PropTech enable outcomes (automation rates, data quality %, AI accuracy).  

---

## 🔗 Relationships Between Elements

- **PVLs → ABOs:** Each Primary Value Lever feeds into one or more aggregated outcomes.  
- **ABOs → BODMs:** Outcomes are measured through business-driven metrics.  
- **BODMs → TODMs:** Each business metric can be enabled or validated by a corresponding technology metric.  

Example Chain:  
- **PVL:** Cost Savings →  
- **ABO:** Enhanced Operational Efficiency & Sustainability →  
- **BODM:** kWh/m² Reduction →  
- **TODM:** Energy Savings % (via AI BMS).

## 🧩 Value Model Canvas (VMC)

A CRE-specific adaptation of Gartner’s **Value Model Canvas** is used to frame use cases:  

- **Outcomes (ABOs)**  
- **Performance Change / Metrics (BODMs)**  
- **Business Process KPIs (Apps/Workflows)**  
- **Data & Metrics (Entities / Attributes; quality notes)**  

This structure helps **trace each outcome to supporting data and workflows**.  

---

## 🔄 Outcome Chains

For specific use cases (e.g., Energy Efficiency, Development Planning, Risk Management), we define **Outcome Chains** that show:  

- **Stakeholders involved**  
- **Desired outcome**  
- **Required process change**  
- **KPIs (BODMs)**  
- **Needed data assets** (and quality considerations)  

This ensures that outcomes are practical and traceable back to data points.  

---

## 📊 Scoring Methodology

To prioritise initiatives, each use case may be scored on:  

- **Value Potential (1–5):** How much business value can be unlocked.  
- **Feasibility (1–5):** Practicality of implementing the change or technology.  
- **Time-to-Value (1–5):** How quickly results can be realised.  
- **Evidence Strength (1–5):** Availability of proof points or case studies.  

**Weighted total = Value Potential × Feasibility × Time-to-Value × Evidence.**  

This allows initiatives to be compared and ranked.

## 📂 Modular Knowledge Base

The knowledge base is structured into separate files to allow modular updating:  

- **ABO_all.md** – Aggregated Business Outcomes  
- **BODM_all.md** – Business Outcome–Driven Metrics  
- **TODM_all.md** – Technology-Outcome Driven Metrics  
- **PVL_all.md** – Primary Value Levers  
- **SUPPLEMENT.md** – Frameworks, methodology, and contextual notes  

This design ensures clarity, avoids duplication, and makes it easy to update one section without breaking the structure.  

---

## 🧾 Notes & Clarifications

- **Atomic Data:** All data points used in TODMs and BODMs are derived from atomic data within Property & Asset Management systems.  
- **Disconnection Principle:** At this stage, atomic data → BODM/TODM mapping is kept separate to maintain flexibility.  
- **Adaptability:** While designed for Industrial & Logistics CRE, the framework is adaptable to Office, Retail, and Residential.
