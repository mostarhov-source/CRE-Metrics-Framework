    # 📑 Business Outcome–Driven Metrics (BODMs)

This file contains a structured knowledge base of Business Outcome–Driven Metrics (BODMs) for Industrial & Logistics Commercial Real Estate (I&L CRE).  
Each metric includes definition, importance, formula, data sources, and example targets/benchmarks.

---

## 💰 Financial Performance

### 1. Internal Rate of Return (IRR)
**Category:** Financial Performance  
**Definition:** The discount rate at which the net present value (NPV) of all future cash flows equals zero.  
**Why It Matters:** Core profitability measure for CRE investments, used to compare projects and set hurdle rates.  
**Formula:** Solve for r in: NPV = Σ [ Ct / (1+r)^t ] = 0  
**Data Sources:** Cash flow forecasts, ERP/Finance, Lease management system.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** 8–12% (core CRE), >15% (opportunistic).  

### 2. Net Present Value (NPV)
**Category:** Financial Performance  
**Definition:** The difference between present value of cash inflows and outflows, discounted at a target rate.  
**Why It Matters:** Determines whether a CRE investment adds value.  
**Formula:** NPV = Σ [ Ct / (1+r)^t ] – C0  
**Data Sources:** Cash flow models, ERP actuals, CAPEX forecasts.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** NPV > 0 indicates value creation.  

### 3. Equity Multiple
**Category:** Financial Performance  
**Definition:** Ratio of total cash returned to total equity invested.  
**Why It Matters:** Simple measure of cash return over holding period.  
**Formula:** Equity Multiple = Total Distributions ÷ Total Equity Invested  
**Data Sources:** ERP, deal models, distribution statements.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** 1.5–2.0x (typical CRE private equity).  

### 4. Loan-to-Value (LTV)
**Category:** Financial Performance  
**Definition:** Ratio of outstanding loan balance to appraised property value.  
**Why It Matters:** Key lender risk metric; impacts refinancing ability.  
**Formula:** LTV = Loan Balance ÷ Property Value  
**Data Sources:** Valuation reports, ERP loan schedules.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≤60% (core), ≤70% (value-add).  

### 5. Debt Yield
**Category:** Financial Performance  
**Definition:** NOI divided by loan amount, showing lender’s risk exposure.  
**Why It Matters:** Indicates ability to service debt independent of cap rates.  
**Formula:** Debt Yield = NOI ÷ Loan Amount  
**Data Sources:** NOI reports, ERP loan data.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥10% typical minimum.  

### 6. Net Operating Income (NOI)
**Category:** Financial Performance  
**Definition:** Income after operating expenses, before financing and taxes.  
**Why It Matters:** Core driver of valuations and investor returns.  
**Formula:** NOI = Rental Income – Operating Expenses  
**Data Sources:** ERP, property management systems.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** NOI growth 2–5% YoY in stable portfolios.

## ⚙️ Operational Efficiency & Risk

### 7. Fault Detection Rate
**Category:** Operational Efficiency  
**Definition:** % of system faults detected by monitoring systems.  
**Why It Matters:** Early detection reduces downtime, risk, and cost.  
**Formula:** Fault Detection Rate = Faults Detected ÷ Total Faults  
**Data Sources:** BMS telemetry, maintenance logs.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥90%.  

### 8. Lease Event Processing Time Reduction %
**Category:** Operational Efficiency  
**Definition:** % reduction in average processing time for lease events.  
**Why It Matters:** Minimises missed deadlines, improves tenant management.  
**Formula:** (Baseline Time – Current Time) ÷ Baseline × 100  
**Data Sources:** Workflow logs, case management.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥30% faster.  

### 9. Vacancy Data Accuracy %
**Category:** Operational Efficiency  
**Definition:** % of reported vacancies matching actual occupancy.  
**Why It Matters:** Accurate vacancy data underpins rent forecasts and valuations.  
**Formula:** Correct Vacancy Records ÷ Total Vacancy Records × 100  
**Data Sources:** Lease DB, CRM, property mgmt.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥98%.  

### 10. Financial Dashboard Automation %
**Category:** Operational Efficiency  
**Definition:** % of dashboards generated automatically from live data.  
**Why It Matters:** Cuts reporting lag and manual workload.  
**Formula:** Automated Dashboards ÷ Total Dashboards × 100  
**Data Sources:** BI tools, ERP.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** 100% core dashboards automated.  

### 11. Asset Hierarchy Data Coverage %
**Category:** Operational Efficiency  
**Definition:** % of properties with complete digital hierarchy (portfolio → property → lease → tenant).  
**Why It Matters:** Ensures visibility and prevents gaps in reporting.  
**Formula:** Fully Digitised Assets ÷ Total Assets × 100  
**Data Sources:** ERP, property management DB.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥95%.  

### 12. Property Data Integration Rate %
**Category:** Operational Efficiency  
**Definition:** % of property-level data sources integrated into central BI/ERP.  
**Why It Matters:** Reduces silos, ensures consistent reporting.  
**Formula:** Integrated Systems ÷ Total Systems × 100  
**Data Sources:** Integration logs, DWH.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥90%.  

### 13. Lease Data Integrity %
**Category:** Operational Efficiency  
**Definition:** % of lease records validated against authoritative sources.  
**Why It Matters:** Ensures accuracy of rent rolls and reporting.  
**Formula:** Validated Lease Records ÷ Total Lease Records × 100  
**Data Sources:** Lease DB, ERP, legal docs.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥95%.  

### 14. Rent Review Automation Rate %
**Category:** Operational Efficiency  
**Definition:** % of rent review processes automated.  
**Why It Matters:** Reduces errors, accelerates cycles.  
**Formula:** Automated Reviews ÷ Total Reviews × 100  
**Data Sources:** Workflow automation logs.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥80%.  

### 15. Report Generation Time Reduction %
**Category:** Operational Efficiency  
**Definition:** % reduction in report production time.  
**Why It Matters:** Frees time for strategic work.  
**Formula:** (Baseline Time – Current Time) ÷ Baseline × 100  
**Data Sources:** Reporting logs, timesheets.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥50%.  

### 16. Tenant Record Duplication Rate %
**Category:** Operational Efficiency  
**Definition:** % of duplicate tenant entries in systems.  
**Why It Matters:** Prevents confusion, improves reporting integrity.  
**Formula:** Duplicate Records ÷ Total Tenant Records × 100  
**Data Sources:** CRM, lease DB.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≤1%.

## 🌍 ESG / Sustainability

### 17. kWh/m² Reduction
**Category:** ESG  
**Definition:** Change in average energy consumption per square metre.  
**Why It Matters:** Tracks efficiency improvements and ESG compliance.  
**Formula:** (Baseline kWh/m² – Current kWh/m²) ÷ Baseline × 100  
**Data Sources:** BMS, energy meters, bills.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥10% reduction.  

### 18. Peak Demand Reduction
**Category:** ESG  
**Definition:** Reduction in maximum energy demand during peak hours.  
**Why It Matters:** Cuts costs and carbon emissions.  
**Formula:** (Baseline Peak Demand – Current) ÷ Baseline × 100  
**Data Sources:** Energy meters, BMS.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥5–10%.  

### 19. Carbon Emissions Reduction
**Category:** ESG  
**Definition:** Reduction in CO₂e emissions from operations.  
**Why It Matters:** ESG compliance and investor reporting.  
**Formula:** (Baseline Emissions – Current) ÷ Baseline × 100  
**Data Sources:** Utility bills, carbon calculators.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** Net Zero by 2040–2050.  

### 20. Waste Reduction %
**Category:** ESG  
**Definition:** % reduction in waste generated.  
**Why It Matters:** Improves ESG scores, cuts disposal costs.  
**Formula:** (Baseline Waste – Current) ÷ Baseline × 100  
**Data Sources:** Waste transfer notes, facility logs.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥25% over 5 years.  

### 21. Green Lease Compliance Automation %
**Category:** ESG  
**Definition:** % of green lease clauses tracked automatically.  
**Why It Matters:** Demonstrates compliance to investors and regulators.  
**Formula:** Automated Clauses ÷ Total Clauses × 100  
**Data Sources:** Lease DB, ESG platform.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥80%.  

### Climate Risk Exposure  
**Definition:** Quantitative assessment of portfolio exposure to physical climate risks such as flooding, heatwaves, and storms.  
**Why It Matters:** Enables targeted risk mitigation and resilience planning to protect asset value and income stability.  
**Formula:** Aggregated risk scores weighted by asset value or footprint.  
**Data Sources:** Climate models, GIS data, insurance claims, asset registries.  
**Example Targets:** Limit high-risk assets to under 10% of portfolio value; reduce projected loss from severe events by 15% over 5 years.

### Embodied Carbon Intensity  
**Definition:** Total embodied carbon emissions per square meter in construction or refurbishment projects.  
**Why It Matters:** Extends sustainability focus beyond operations to lifecycle impacts, supporting net-zero goals.  
**Formula:**  
\[
\frac{\text{Total Embodied Carbon (kg CO₂e)}}{\text{Gross Floor Area (m}^2\text{)}}
\]  
**Data Sources:** Design data, life-cycle assessment databases, construction records.  
**Example Targets:** 20% reduction in embodied carbon over 5 years.

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Tenant / Customer Outcomes

### 22. Tenant Retention Rate
**Category:** Tenant Outcomes  
**Definition:** % of tenants renewing leases vs total expiring leases.  
**Why It Matters:** Critical to stabilising NOI.  
**Formula:** Renewed Leases ÷ Expiring Leases × 100  
**Data Sources:** Lease DB, CRM.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** ≥80%.  

### 23. Tenant Payment Reliability Index (TPRI)
**Category:** Tenant Outcomes  
**Definition:** Composite index of overdue receivables, days past due, and default rate.  
**Why It Matters:** Forward indicator of credit health and NOI resilience.  
**Formula:** 100 – (0.4×Overdue% + 0.3×Avg DPD + 0.3×Default Rate)  
**Data Sources:** ERP, finance, tenant CRM.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** Target ≥85; intervention <70.  

### 24. Average Daily Rate (ADR)
**Category:** Tenant Outcomes  
**Definition:** Average rental revenue per day per unit (mainly flex-industrial).  
**Why It Matters:** Benchmark for short-term/flex assets.  
**Formula:** ADR = Total Rental Revenue ÷ Total Unit-Days Sold  
**Data Sources:** Booking/reservation system, ERP.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** Benchmarked against market ADR.  

### 25. Market Rent vs Contract Rent
**Category:** Tenant Outcomes  
**Definition:** Ratio of contract rent to market rent.  
**Why It Matters:** Identifies under- or over-rented units.  
**Formula:** Contract Rent ÷ Market Rent × 100  
**Data Sources:** Lease DB, market comps.  
**Example Target / Benchmarks:** 90–110%.

## Financial Performance

### Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)  
**Definition:** Average capital cost weighted by debt and equity proportions used as a hurdle rate in investment decisions.  
**Why It Matters:** Influences portfolio valuation and investment return expectations.  
**Formula:**  
\[
\text{WACC} = \frac{E}{V} \times Re + \frac{D}{V} \times Rd \times (1-T_c)
\]  
(E=Equity, D=Debt, V=E+D, Re=Cost of Equity, Rd=Cost of Debt, Tc=Tax Rate)  
**Data Sources:** Financial statements, debt agreements, market data.  
**Example Targets:** WACC below 7% for core portfolio investments.

## Operational Efficiency

### Cybersecurity Incident Rate  
**Definition:** Number of detected cybersecurity breaches or fraud incidents in CRE IT systems annually.  
**Why It Matters:** Reduces risk of operational disruption, data loss, and reputational damage.  
**Formula:**  
\[
\frac{\text{Number of Incidents}}{\text{Total Systems Monitored}} \times 100
\]  
**Data Sources:** Security logs, audit trails, incident reports.  
**Example Targets:** Fewer than 1 incident per 100 monitored systems annually; ongoing improvement in vulnerability patching.

## Market Performance

### Market Absorption Rate  
**Definition:** Percentage of available leasable or saleable space absorbed in a market or portfolio over a specified period.  
**Why It Matters:** Indicates market demand and leasing success critical for revenue growth.  
**Formula:**  
\[
\frac{\text{Leased or Sold Area}}{\text{Total Available Area}} \times 100
\]  
**Data Sources:** Lease management systems, brokerage reports, CRM databases.  
**Example Targets:** 85% absorption within 12 months in core markets.

---

## Tenant Customer Outcomes

### Tenant Utilization Rate  
**Definition:** Proportion of leased space actively used by tenants, reflecting changes due to hybrid and flexible working.  
**Why It Matters:** Assesses space efficiency and tenant satisfaction, guiding portfolio optimization.  
**Formula:**  
\[
\frac{\text{Actual Occupied Space}}{\text{Leased Space}} \times 100
\]  
**Data Sources:** Occupancy sensors, access control, tenant reports.  
**Example Targets:** Over 75% utilization in flexible office spaces.
