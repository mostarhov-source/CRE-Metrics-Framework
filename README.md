# CRE Metrics Framework

A comprehensive framework for measuring business value and outcomes in Commercial Real Estate (CRE), with a focus on PropTech and AI investments.

## Overview

The CRE Metrics Framework provides a structured approach to connecting technology investments to business value through a four-level hierarchy:

- **Primary Value Levers (PVL)** - Fundamental drivers of value creation
- **Aggregated Business Outcomes (ABO)** - Strategic outcomes organizations aim to achieve  
- **Business Outcome-Driven Metrics (BODM)** - Metrics that measure business outcomes
- **Technology-Outcome Driven Metrics (TODM)** - Technical measures of how systems enable outcomes

This framework is designed for Industrial & Logistics CRE but is adaptable to Office, Retail, and Residential sectors.

---

## Why This Framework?

**The Problem:**  
Most PropTech and AI case studies claim "success" but fail to connect technology deployment to measurable business value. This creates:

- Unclear ROI justification
- Difficulty comparing solutions
- Inability to track value realization
- Disconnect between technical metrics and business outcomes

**The Solution:**  
This framework provides a standardized way to:

- Map technology investments to business value
- Compare case studies consistently
- Identify which value levers drive which outcomes
- Trace the complete value chain from tech to portfolio value

## How to Use This Framework

### For Analyzing Case Studies

1. **Identify the Primary Value Lever (PVL)**  
   Which fundamental driver of value is being addressed? (Revenue Growth, Cost Savings, Risk Reduction, ESG, or Productivity)

2. **Map to Aggregated Business Outcome (ABO)**  
   What strategic outcome is the organization trying to achieve?

3. **Extract Business Metrics (BODM)**  
   Which business metrics moved? By how much? (e.g., IRR improved from 8% to 12%)

4. **Document Technology Metrics (TODM)**  
   What technical capabilities enabled the outcome? (e.g., 23% Energy Savings via AI-powered BMS)

5. **Calculate Financial Impact**  
   - Annual Benefit from Operations (ABO)
   - Portfolio Value Lift (PVL)

### Example Value Chain
```

PVL: Cost Savings
  ↓
ABO: Enhanced Operational Efficiency & Sustainability
  ↓
BODM: kWh/m² reduced by 23% (180 → 138)
      Carbon intensity reduced by 18%
  ↓
TODM: Energy Savings % = 23%
      Data Capture Automation = 85%
  ↓
Financial Impact: 
      ABO = £2.3M annual savings
      PVL = £45M portfolio value increase (green premium)
```

## Framework Principles

### Atomic Data Foundation

All metrics are derived from atomic data points in property and asset management systems.

### Outcome-First Approach  

Start with desired business outcomes, then identify the metrics and technology needed to achieve them.

### Modular Design

Each component (PVL, ABO, BODM, TODM) is independent and can be updated without breaking the framework.

### Evidence-Based

Metrics include example benchmarks, targets, and real-world evidence from the industry.

### Adaptable

While designed for Industrial & Logistics CRE, the framework can be adapted to other asset classes.
